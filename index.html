Our Story

A token born from gratitude.

Before there were charts, candles, and roadmaps, there was her. The first hand we ever held. The voice that softened every storm. The quiet hero who built the people we became, one meal, one hug, one sleepless night at a time.

$MOMS was created as a bouquet the blockchain could carry forever. A digital flower that never wilts. A way for our community to celebrate the most important woman in our lives, and uplift the mothers still fighting to give their children a beautiful tomorrow.

Behind every great person is a mother who believed in them first.
